Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

About This Application

Cylinder Text Animation ("the application") is a browser-based tool for creating and previewing animated text on a cylinder. The application processes text and settings locally and does not require an account or any other personal information.

Information and Data We Process

The text, animation settings, font choices, and optional border images you select are used only to render the animation in your browser. They are not sent to us or to any third party. The application does not collect names, email addresses, account details, location data, or device identifiers.

Saved Presets

If you save a preset, its settings and any selected border image are stored in your browser's local storage on this device. This data remains under your control: you can delete a preset in the application or remove all application data through your browser's site settings. Presets are not synchronized or transmitted to us.

Installed Fonts

When you choose to load installed fonts, the application uses your browser's Local Font Access capability to list font names for use in the animation. It may also read the font data needed to measure and display your text. This operation occurs locally, and we do not receive font information or other device data. Font access is optional and depends on browser support and permission.

Cookies, Analytics, and Third Parties

The application does not use cookies, tracking technologies, advertising, or analytics scripts. It does not sell or share personal information and does not send your animation, text, settings, or presets to a server or third party.

Hosting and Network Connections

When the application is loaded from a website, that website's host may receive ordinary HTTP request information, such as the requested page and technical information included in browser requests. Local file use does not involve a host. Any such hosting-provider information is governed by the hosting website's own privacy practices, which are outside this policy.

Data Security and Retention

The last-used settings and any selected border image are retained in browser local storage automatically. Saved presets, including their images, remain there until you delete them or clear the site's data. JSON exports are created only when you request them and include any selected border image. Because processing is local, we do not maintain a copy of your application data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements. The revised policy will be included with the application and identified by its updated date.

Contact

Questions or concerns about this policy may be directed through the application's repository or distribution page.
